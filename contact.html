Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity? +

For most products like mugs, T-shirts, and keychains, the minimum order is 1 piece. For bulk discounts, we recommend ordering 50+ pieces. Banners and posters can be ordered in single quantities.

How much time does it take to deliver? +

Standard delivery takes 2–4 working days after design approval. Express same-day and next-day delivery is available for urgent orders at extra charges. Bulk orders may take 5–7 working days.

Can you help with design? +

Yes! We have an in-house design team. We provide free design assistance with your order. Just share your idea, logo, and text and we'll create a professional design for your approval before printing.

Do you provide bulk discounts? +

Yes, we offer attractive discounts for bulk orders. For 50+ pieces you get 10% off, for 100+ pieces 20% off, and for 500+ pieces we offer custom pricing. Contact us for a bulk order quote.

What file formats do you accept for designs? +

We accept PDF, AI, CDR, PSD, PNG (high-res), JPG (300 DPI+). For best quality, vector files (AI, PDF, CDR) are preferred. We can also work from photos and rough sketches.

Do you deliver outside Delhi? +

Yes! We deliver pan-India through courier partners. Delivery charges depend on location and weight. Delhi NCR orders can also be picked up from our store for free.
